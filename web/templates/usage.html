Shared key pressure is the highest quota usage (percent of a provider limit already consumed) among the shared keys a client used. High pressure means several clients may be draining the same upstream key.

Refreshable keys are tokens Duckway renews itself with a refresh token, so they do not need to be replaced by hand when they expire.

Unpriced requests used a model that has no price configured on its service, so their tokens are counted but their cost is not.
